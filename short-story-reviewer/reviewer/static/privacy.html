What happens to your story

  • Your story, and the file you upload, are sent to this server inside the request that runs the edit.
  • The server processes them in memory. It does not write them to disk, store them in a database or log their text. Server logs record that a request happened, not what it contained.
  • When the run ends, or if you close the page, the story is discarded. The letter and the annotated manuscript are sent back to your browser and are not kept on the server.
  • Nothing about your story is used to train anything by this app.

Anthropic

To be edited, the story is sent to Anthropic's API, which runs the Claude models that do the reading. Anthropic handles API data under its own commercial terms and privacy policy; check those for how long it keeps API inputs and outputs.

Your browser

The page keeps your story in the browser tab while you work. Closing or reloading the tab clears it.

Rate limits

To keep costs down, the server counts requests per network address for an hour at a time. It keeps the count, not the content.

Markets

Market facts (word limits and AI policies) are quoted from each market's own guidelines, with the date they were checked. Guidelines change; check the market's page before you submit.
