# Method files go here

The app reads these files by name:

| File | Needed | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `Short story editorial method and checklist.md` | **Yes** | The system prompt of every model call. Runs are switched off until it is here. |
| `Short story craft and editing.md` | Recommended | Reference. Only matching sections are loaded: headings mentioning speculative fiction, science fiction or fantasy go to the genre layer; comic, humour, deadpan, dark, register or tone go to the register layer. |
| `Example editorial letter - Sorcerer Soldier.md` | Optional | Sent to the letter stage as a model of shape and tone only, with an instruction not to copy its content or wording. |

Do not put any of the writer's own stories here, and never add the source
books the research drew on.
